You are planning the scenes of a cinematic video.

Convert the following story into a structured sequence of scenes.

Each scene represents one continuous story section that may later
contain multiple shots.

Do not generate video prompts.
Do not generate shots.
Do not add information outside the JSON structure.

STORY:

{story}

CHARACTERS:

{characters}

Return ONLY valid JSON in exactly this format:

{
  "scenes": [
    {
      "scene_id": "scene_001",
      "order": 1,
      "location": "",
      "time_of_day": "",
      "description": "",
      "mood": "",
      "lighting": "",
      "characters": [],
      "story_summary": "",
      "continuity_notes": "",
      "shot_ids": []
    }
  ]
}

FIELD RULES:

scene_id:
Use sequential IDs:
scene_001
scene_002
scene_003

order:
Use sequential integers starting from 1.

location:
Describe the physical location of the scene.

time_of_day:
Examples:
morning
afternoon
evening
night
sunrise
sunset

description:
Describe the overall visual situation and environment of the scene.
Do not describe individual camera shots.

mood:
Describe the dominant emotional and cinematic atmosphere of the scene.
Examples:
ominous
mysterious
isolated
tense
melancholic
wonderous
threatening

lighting:
Describe the scene's lighting conditions and visual character.
Examples:
cold blue moonlight
dim volumetric light
warm sunset light
flickering torchlight
diffuse misty light
high-contrast directional light

characters:
List only character names present in the scene.

story_summary:
Summarize what happens in this scene.

continuity_notes:
Describe information that must remain consistent when the next
scene begins, including character state, clothing, objects,
location changes, injuries, emotional state, or important actions.

shot_ids:
Return an empty list [].
Shot IDs will be created later by the shot planning stage.

IMPORTANT:

- Return valid JSON only.
- Do not use Markdown.
- Do not wrap the JSON in ``` blocks.
- Do not include explanations before or after the JSON.
- Keep character names consistent with the provided character data.
- Do not invent new major characters unless required by the story.
- Preserve the logical order of events.
- Keep mood and lighting consistent with the story and location.
- Do not introduce lighting that contradicts the time of day.
