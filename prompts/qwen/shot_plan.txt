SHOT RULES:

Each shot should represent one clear cinematic beat.

Do not cram unrelated actions into one shot.

Use the same character identity established in the character data.

REFERENCE IDENTITY:

Reference images establish:
- face
- hair
- hairline
- body structure
- body proportions
- stable identity features

Do not change these.

QWEN MAY DETERMINE:

- clothing
- environment
- lighting
- props
- scars

- emotional state
- temporary physical state

But these must remain consistent once introduced.

A new scar or injury may only appear when logically introduced by the story.

CAMERA:

- subject framing

AUDIO:

Describe:
- dialogue
- voice delivery
- ambience
- effects
- music

Dialogue should be represented clearly.
